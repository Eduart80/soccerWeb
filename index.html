EAGLE STARS SOCCER ACADEMY

We are not just another soccer program. We are a skills-only development academy built for kids who want to master the ball, play with confidence, and grow step by step.

Our Training Identity

Every session is designed to help players feel comfortable with the ball under pressure and use their skills in real game situations.

Ball Mastery

First touch, close control, both feet, turns, fakes, change of direction, and ball confidence.

Game Intelligence

Scanning, awareness, body position, decision-making, and learning when to dribble, pass, or protect the ball.

Shielding & Confidence

Players learn to use their body, arms, and balance to protect the ball and stay calm under pressure.

Real 1v1 Training

Attacking and defending situations that build courage, creativity, and real match problem-solving.
