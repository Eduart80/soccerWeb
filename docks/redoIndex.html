Meet the Founder

Klaudio Aliaj

Founder & Director of Coaching

With over a decade of professional coaching experience, Klaudio Aliaj built Eagle Stars Soccer Academy on one belief: every child deserves world-class coaching. His player-first approach has shaped hundreds of athletes, helping them master the technical, tactical, and mental sides of the beautiful game.

Klaudio's coaching philosophy is simple — develop the whole player. On and off the field, Eagle Stars athletes learn discipline, leadership, and how to compete at the highest level.

Training Programs

⚽
Ages 3–6

Little Eagles

Introduction to soccer through fun, age-appropriate drills. Build coordination, love for the game, and early technical skills in a safe environment.

🦅
Ages 7–12

Rising Stars

Core skill development — dribbling, shooting, passing, positioning. Players compete in local leagues and begin understanding team tactics.

Elite Academy

High-performance training for competitive players. Advanced tactics, fitness conditioning, and college/academy placement guidance.

One-on-one sessions with Coach Klaudio. Personalized curriculum focused on your specific weaknesses and goals. Fast-track your development.
