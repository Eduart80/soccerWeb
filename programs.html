CORE DEVELOPMENT PILLARS
1

BALL MASTERY

  • Close control (tight spaces)
  • Dribbling with both feet
  • Change of direction
  • Turns, fakes, body feints
  • First touch under pressure

GOAL: Player never panics on the ball

2
👁️

GAME AWARENESS (SCANNING)

  • Shoulder checks before receiving
  • Awareness of pressure & space
  • Knowing next action early
  • Body positioning when receiving

GOAL: Player plays faster without rushing

3
🧠

GAME INTELLIGENCE

  • When to pass, dribble, or turn
  • Recognizing overloads & switches
  • Playing out of pressure
  • Positioning between lines

GOAL: Smart players, not robotic players

4
1v1

1v1 DOMINANCE

  • Confidence in attacking and defending duels
  • Beating players off the dribble
  • Protecting the ball (shielding)
  • Defensive body positioning
  • Timing tackles

GOAL: Win your moment

5
✓

DECISION MAKING

  • Training the brain, not just the feet
  • Quick decisions under pressure
  • Choosing simple vs risky actions
  • Playing forward vs safe

GOAL: Right decision.

8-WEEK DEVELOPMENT CYCLE
WEEKS 1–2

Ball Mastery Foundation — Control, both feet, confidence, basic turns & movement

WEEKS 3–4

Change of Direction & 1v1 — Beating defenders, protecting the ball

WEEKS 5–6

Scanning & Awareness — Playing with head up, receiving under pressure

WEEKS 7–8

Decision Making & Game Intelligence — Small-sided games, real game scenarios
