Klaudio leads the soccer development side of Eagle Stars Soccer Academy. With years of playing and coaching experience, his focus is simple: help every player build confidence, improve their ball mastery, and understand the game better. His training approach is centered on fundamentals, creativity, discipline, and long-term development — helping players grow step by step, from their first touch to real game situations.

Lulzim helps lead the operational side of Eagle Stars Soccer Academy, making sure the academy runs with organization, professionalism, and care for families. His role supports the structure behind the training environment — communication, planning, coordination, and creating a smooth experience for players and parents.

Our Leadership

Eagle Stars Soccer Academy is built by people who care about player development, family trust, and creating the right environment for kids to grow.

Together

Together, Klaudio and Lulzim are building a skills-based soccer academy where players can start small, dream big, and become great through hard work, confidence, and proper development
